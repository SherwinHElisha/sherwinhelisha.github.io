THE PREMISE

## An ancient mystery, finally legible.

For centuries, the sixth bowl of Revelation was read as symbol — a statue of clay and iron, a mark no one could buy or sell without, spirits like frogs leaping from a dragon's mouth. Generations debated it. Few could picture it.

Then came the convergence: surveillance woven into daily life, biometric systems replacing cash, algorithms that spread a message farther and faster than any voice in history. The old prophecy stopped reading like myth and started reading like a headline.

The Sixth Bowl series follows that convergence — and the question every watching generation eventually has to answer.

EDITION ONE

## The Gemini-Coauthored Trilogy

Same prompts. Same chapter architecture. Interpreted through Gemini as co-author.

The statue in Daniel's dream had feet of iron and clay — strength and fragility fused into one empire. Today, that fusion has a name: total surveillance, one hand at a time. When the ancient prophecy starts reading like the headlines, one man has to decide whether he's watching history repeat — or watching it end.

For centuries, a mark no one could buy or sell without seemed impossible to imagine. Then came biometric payments, digital currency, and patents already filed to track a person through their own body's data. A Mark, Blood, and Testimony follows what happens when identity itself becomes the currency — and refusing the mark becomes the most dangerous decision a person can make.

Unclean spirits like frogs, leaping from a dragon's mouth — for centuries it read as strange symbolism, hard to picture. Then came algorithms built to spread a message instantly into every home on earth. The Dragon's Mouth closes the trilogy where deception meets its convergence, when every voice, every platform, and every spirit align toward one final push.
